Import your history

Spotify's API only shows your last 50 plays. Your full history is in Spotify's privacy export, and importing it fills your lifetime stats right away.

1. Request your data

  1. Open spotify.com/account/privacy ↗ and sign in.
  2. Tick Extended streaming history (every play since you joined, takes up to 30 days) or Account data (the last year, takes about 5 days).
  3. Confirm the email Spotify sends, then wait for the download link.
  4. Unzip the download.

2. Pick the files

Choose every Streaming_History_Audio_*.json (extended) or StreamingHistory_music_*.json (account data) file. Other files are ignored. Plays you already have here are skipped, so importing twice is safe.
